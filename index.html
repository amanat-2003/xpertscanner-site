XpertScanner

A modern document scanner for Android. Scan paper documents, organise them in folders, search their text, chat with them using AI, and export them as PDF.

Features

Scan with the camera, edit and reorder pages
Folders, thumbnails and on-device text search (OCR)
AI assistant to ask questions about a scan
Export to PDF with quality presets and optional password
Signatures you can save and place on documents
Backup to your own Google Drive
