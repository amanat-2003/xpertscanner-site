Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what data the XpertScanner app ("the app") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is provided by Amanat Singh ("we", "us").

Data we collect

  • Account information: your email address, name and profile picture when you sign in with Google, Apple or email. Authentication is handled by Supabase.
  • Scans and documents: images you capture or import, and text recognised from them. These are stored on your device. Text recognition (OCR) runs on your device.
  • Scan metadata: names, folder structure and timestamps, stored in our Supabase database so your library syncs with your account.
  • Usage counts: the number of AI requests you make per day, to apply the free daily limit.

Camera and storage permissions

The app asks for camera access to scan documents and for storage or photo access to import and save files. These are used only for those features.

Google user data and Google Drive

If you turn on Google Drive backup, the app requests access to Google Drive using the drive.file and drive.appdata scopes. These let the app create and access only the files it creates itself and its own hidden app-data folder. It cannot see or read your other Drive files.

  • We use this access only to back up and restore your scans in your own Drive.
  • Backup files go straight from your device to your Google Drive. We do not copy them to our servers.
  • We do not sell, share or use Google user data for advertising.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI assistant

When you use the AI assistant on a scan, the text of that scan and your question are sent to an AI provider (Google Gemini, or an OpenAI-compatible service) to generate an answer. This happens only when you ask a question. Do not use the assistant on documents you do not want processed this way.

Who we share data with

  • Supabase: authentication and database hosting.
  • Google: sign-in, Drive backup, and the Gemini AI service.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete scans inside the app at any time, and delete backup files from your Google Drive. You can revoke the app's Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete your account and the data we hold, email us at the address below and we will remove it.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections, and our database uses row-level security so each user can only access their own records. No method of storage is perfectly secure, however.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
